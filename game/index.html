A solo text adventure

Dicebound

Make a hero. Type what you do. The dice decide the rest.

How to play
  • Type anything. "I sneak past the guard", "I try to lift the portcullis", "I ask the innkeeper about the barrow." There are no menus to pick from.
  • Risky things get a roll. The DM sets a difficulty (DC) for the task. You roll a d20 and add your modifier for the skill. Meet or beat the DC and you succeed.
  • Your character matters. A wizard can try to bend iron bars — but with a weak Strength they'll probably fail. Play to your strengths.
  • A quiet start. The first 10–15 turns are calm: explore, talk, find your feet. After that, something happens every few turns — fights, traps, strangers, treasure.
  • Level up. Earn XP from fights, clever checks and good roleplay. Levels bring hit points, new powers and ability boosts. Bosses tied to your quest appear at levels 4, 7 and 10.
  • Death is real. Drop to 0 HP and you roll death saves. Three failures and your story ends.

      The Dungeon Master

      Dicebound can use Claude as your Dungeon Master — it understands anything you type, voices characters and remembers your story. Without it, a built-in offline DM runs the game with simpler, keyword-based storytelling. The dice and rules are identical either way.

      Your key is stored only in this browser (localStorage) and sent only to Anthropic's API. Each turn makes two requests, billed to your key. Get a key at console.anthropic.com. Don't use this on a shared computer.
